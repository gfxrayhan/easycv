Frequently asked questions

Quick answers before you start.

Is EasyCV free?

Yes. You can use all three tools without paying and without creating an account.

Do you save or upload my information?

This website does not upload your CV, photos or resume text to a server. Everything is processed in your browser. If auto-save is on, your CV form (including a photo or signature you added) is kept in this browser on this device only, so you can continue later. You can turn auto-save off or delete saved data at any time using the "Privacy & saved data" link. Helper libraries for PDF, Word and ZIP files are downloaded from public CDNs (jsDelivr or cdnjs) the first time you use those features, and the hosting service may keep normal server logs.

Which formats can I download?

CVs: PDF (recommended), Print / Save as PDF (sharpest text), Word (.docx), JPEG and PNG. Long CVs are exported page by page, and image exports of several pages come as one ZIP file. Passport photos: JPEG and PNG at 300 DPI, plus a print sheet as PDF, JPEG or PNG.

Is the text in the PDF selectable and readable by ATS?

The PDF download looks exactly like the preview and includes a hidden text layer and clickable links, so search and most ATS can read it. That hidden layer covers Latin letters and numbers only. Text in other scripts, such as Bangla, is visible in the PDF but may not be searchable. For fully selectable text in any script, use "Print / Save as PDF" or the Word file. You can test any file with the ATS Resume Checker.

Can I make a Europass style CV?

Yes. Two of the templates, Europass and Europass Band, follow the Europass style. You can change their colour too.

Is the ATS score real?

No. It is an estimate based on common ATS rules: readable text, clear sections, contact details, bullet points, measurable results and keywords. Every company uses a different ATS and most do not show candidates a score, so no tool can promise an exact result. Use it as a guide to improve your CV.

How does the passport photo background remover work?

Auto subject looks at the colours and the position of the person in the photo, so it works best with a plain background. It is not an AI model. You can fix any spots with the Erase and Restore brushes, and use Spot heal for small marks. Check the size rules of the office you are applying to before you print.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes. It works on phones, tablets and laptops. A few extras, like the close-up preview for Spot heal, are shown on larger screens.

What happens when my CV is longer than one page?

Pages are split automatically, and lines or sections are not cut in half. The preview shows each page separately.

Privacy & saved data

  • Your files stay on your device. Your CV, photos, signature and resume text are processed in your browser. This website does not upload them to a server, and the code uses no accounts, analytics or cookies.
  • What is saved: if auto-save is on, your CV form (including a photo or signature you added) is stored in this browser's local storage on this device only. Passport photos and ATS text are not saved.
  • What loads from the internet: PDF, Word, ZIP and PDF-reading helper libraries load from public CDNs (jsDelivr or cdnjs) the first time you use those features. They see your IP address and which library was requested, never your documents. The hosting service may also keep normal server logs.
  • Shared computer? Turn auto-save off or delete saved data when you finish.
